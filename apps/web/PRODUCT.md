# Product

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

[README](../../README.md) owns household jobs, entity meaning and binding tenets.
Cubby's design serves a small trusted household through desktop web, native
Apple apps in the physical home, and agents over MCP. Dense browsing,
comparison, editing, planning and reconciliation are desktop priorities;
fieldwork belongs to native apps.

## Positioning

The distinguishing connection is recipes to actual products, approximate
stock, prices and unit mappings, alongside household projects connected to
tasks and spending. Design should make that connected record useful while
keeping maintenance proportionate to a personal tool.

## Brand commitments

Use **Cubby**, direct practical language and a household tone. This is a daily
utility, not marketing for strangers. The mark lives in `public/favicon.svg`.
The repository has no approved testimonials, customer claims or public
benchmarks; use synthetic content under [agent rules](../../AGENTS.md).

## Design evidence

- [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) owns web appearance and interaction;
  [native design](../apple/DESIGN.md) owns the Apple experience.
- [Terminology](../../docs/guide/terminology.md) owns domain language.
- [Compiled entities](../../docs/entities.md) owns generic presentation.
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/nickysemenza/cubby/issues) owns future work; existing routes are evidence
  of implementation, not of installed-client acceptance.

## Product principles

Preserve useful estimates, deliberate stock changes, complete work/spend
history, readable identity and truthful unknowns. The README tenets govern
behavior; design makes those choices understandable without inventing another
product contract here.
