# Naming Proposals for RecipeHub

## Top 5 Name Candidates

### 1. cubby

- **Strengths**: Perfect household storage feel, works for both inventory and recipes, friendly and memorable, specific enough to not be generic
- **Usage**: "Recipe cubby", "pantry cubby", "garage cubby" - natural bridge between recipes and inventory
- **Feel**: Cozy, personal, organized storage spaces that everyone understands

### 2. stash

- **Strengths**: Captures the "where did I stash that?" problem the app solves, natural household vocabulary, simple and clear
- **Usage**: "I stashed it somewhere" - exactly the problem being solved
- **Feel**: Casual, household-appropriate, action-oriented

### 3. shelf

- **Strengths**: Aligns with how people naturally organize (pantry shelf, garage shelf), matches the locations architecture perfectly
- **Usage**: People think in terms of shelves across all rooms and storage areas
- **Feel**: Clean, organizational, universally understood

### 4. stocky

- **Strengths**: Catchy and modern feel, clearly inventory-related, memorable
- **Usage**: Friendly take on "stock" with personality
- **Feel**: Approachable, slightly playful while still functional

### 5. cache

- **Strengths**: Suggests stored/hidden items, fits the household discovery problem, less common than basic storage words
- **Usage**: "Cache of items" - implies organized but somewhat hidden storage
- **Feel**: Slightly more sophisticated, tech-friendly but not overly technical

## Context

- System is for household inventory management across any space (garage, bookshelf, pantry, fridge)
- Includes both item tracking and recipe management
- Target: household/personal use only
- Preference: simple, not overly generic, works for both inventory and recipes

## Additional Candidates (Food/Measure Hint)

### tare

- **Strengths**: Precise, measurement-forward (scale/taring), great for inventory amounts; subtle kitchen tie-in
- **Usage**: "Tare it and track it"; fits both recipes and household quantities
- **Feel**: Clean, technical-but-friendly; short and distinct

### ration

- **Strengths**: Clear portioning/stock vibe; broadly applicable to pantry and general supplies
- **Usage**: "Ration out a pack", "what's our ration left?"
- **Feel**: Measured, practical, slightly vintage

### pinch

- **Strengths**: Native recipe micro-measure; playful and memorable while implying precision
- **Usage**: "Add a pinch" → small amounts, works metaphorically for items
- **Feel**: Approachable, warm, culinary hint without being niche

### trug

- **Strengths**: Garden carry-basket metaphor; gathering and organizing across contexts
- **Usage**: "Drop it in the trug"; natural collection container
- **Feel**: Earthy, practical, slightly rustic; adjacent to household organization
