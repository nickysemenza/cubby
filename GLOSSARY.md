# Household taxonomy

Vocabulary for household classification. Cross-layer domain terminology lives in
[Terminology](docs/guide/terminology.md).

## Storage locations

**Backstock**:
Reserve supplies held separately from everyday access or active use. An explicit
location label or note records this role; storing an item in a bin does not by
itself establish backstock. A location can remain physically present when empty.

Location names describe identifiable physical places or the recorded contents
of an existing container. A plausible destination based on an item's category
does not establish where the item is physically stored.

## Project scope

**Standing household project**:
An ongoing grouping for routine household consumption, including food, personal
care, and pet supplies. It remains open across individual purchases. Bounded
work, events, and trips keep their own Projects when the records identify them.

## Compatibility tags

**Compatibility tag**:
A reusable battery platform, mounting interface, fit standard, or product
ecosystem used to select compatible equipment and replacements. Its usefulness
does not depend on how many Products currently carry it. Manufacturer names,
purchase provenance, and descriptive classifications use their own fields.
An equipment maker named on a third-party fitting can identify its target
system; distinguish that fit information from the fitting's own manufacturer.

## Garden planting materials

**Seeds**:
Botanical seeds sold for sowing, including vegetable, herb, flower, and cover-crop
seed. Seed-starting equipment remains separate.

**Plants & planting stock**:
Plants, starts, trees, sod, bulbs, corms, and planting cloves. Botanical seeds
belong with Seeds.

## Kitchen

**Cookware & bakeware**:
Reusable vessels, trays, molds, racks, and cooking surfaces used to cook or bake
food, including their fitted lids. Paper liners and other expendable materials
are Consumables.

**Utensils & prep tools**:
Reusable implements for preparing, measuring, handling, and finishing food or
drinks, including knives, cutting boards, strainers, and manual brewing tools.

**Tableware & drinkware**:
Reusable dishes, serving vessels, eating utensils, and drinking vessels used to
serve or consume food and drinks.

**Consumables (Kitchen)**:
Expendable materials used to prepare, preserve, serve, or clean up food, including
wraps, paper filters, disposable serviceware, gas chargers, and cutting-board
care products.

## Pantry staples

**Grains & pasta**:
Rice, other grains, pasta, and noodle products used as meal staples. Breakfast
cereals and oats have their own group.

**Beans & lentils**:
Dried, canned, or prepared pulses, including beans, lentils, chickpeas, and split
peas.

**Baking & specialty ingredients**:
Flours, sweeteners, leaveners, baking mixes, and specialty food ingredients used
to control texture, preservation, or fermentation, including nut flours and
baking pastes.

**Nuts & seeds**:
Whole, chopped, or ground nuts and seeds, plus shredded coconut, used directly as
food or ingredients. Nut flours and baking pastes belong with Baking & specialty
ingredients; ready-to-use spreads belong with Nut & seed butters.

**Bread & tortillas**:
Ready-made breads, bagels, buns, pita, tortillas, and unleavened breads.

**Breakfast cereals & oats**:
Breakfast cereals, granola, oatmeal, and rolled oats, including flavored and
fortified varieties.

## Produce

**Fruit**:
Culinary fruits eaten as fruit, including berries, citrus, melons, avocados,
plantains, and dates. Tomatoes and peppers belong with Vegetables.

**Leafy greens**:
Leaf vegetables and salad mixes, including lettuce, spinach, kale, and cabbage.

**Roots & alliums**:
Root vegetables, tubers, bulbs, and alliums, including potatoes, carrots, onions,
garlic, leeks, scallions, and chives.

**Vegetables**:
Produce used as vegetables outside Leafy greens and Roots & alliums, including
tomatoes, peppers, squash, legumes sold as vegetables, and mushrooms.

**Herbs**:
Culinary herb leaves, sprigs, and bunches, including basil, cilantro, mint,
parsley, rosemary, and thyme. Live potted plants remain distinct from cut herbs.

## Hand tools

Hand-operated implements and portable measuring or testing instruments for
construction, repair, and maintenance, including reusable drain-clearing and
fitting-removal tools.

**Garden tools**:
Hand-operated implements for digging, planting, pruning, harvesting, and raking.
These retain tool behavior; plants and growing materials are separate supplies.

**Hammers & striking**:
Hammers, mallets, and punches used to deliver or receive a striking force.

**Measuring, marking & test**:
Portable instruments for measuring, laying out, marking, inspecting, or testing
work, including levels, rules, gauges, and electrical testers.

**Clamps & vises**:
Reusable tools that hold workpieces in position, including workholding magnets
and fitted clamp accessories.

**Cutting & surface finishing**:
Hand-operated cutting, scraping, shaping, sharpening, and finishing tools,
including saws, knives, chisels, files, trowels, and floats.

**Driving & gripping**:
Screwdrivers, keys, sockets, wrenches, pliers, and crimpers used to turn,
grip, or compress work and fasteners.

## Fasteners

**Anchors**:
Fasteners that secure an attachment through engagement with its supporting
material, including masonry anchors, wall plugs, and toggles.

**Screws**:
Threaded screw fasteners, including wood, sheet-metal, machine, and socket-head
screws. Nuts sold as machine-screw nuts belong with Bolts, nuts & washers.

**Bolts, nuts & washers**:
Bolts, threaded nuts, and fastening washers, including coupling nuts and
threaded lifting eyes. Plumbing seal assortments remain distinct.

**Nails & staples**:
Driven nails, brads, pins, and general fastening staples, including collated
products. Cable supports and landscaping staples remain distinct.

**Connectors & supports**:
Brackets, straps, ties, threaded rods, and links used to connect or support
construction materials and mounting assemblies.

## Electrical

**Boxes & enclosures**:
Electrical installation boxes, junction enclosures, extension rings, mud rings,
blank box covers, and knockout seals.

**Devices & plates**:
Electrical switches, dimmers, receptacles, circuit breakers, and their exposed
faceplates and protective covers.

**Wire & cable**:
Conductors and assembled electrical cable. Empty routing conduit and separate
connection, insulation, or securing materials have their own groups.

**Conduit & raceway**:
Empty protective electrical routing, together with its connectors, couplings,
elbows, bodies, and mounting fittings.

**Wiring accessories**:
Materials that connect, insulate, secure, protect, or help install wiring,
including splicing connectors, cable clips, grommets, tape, and heat-shrink tubing.

## Plumbing

**Drainage & sewer**:
Drain, waste, vent, and stormwater components, including traps, drain fittings,
perforated landscaping drainpipe, catch basins, and strainers.

**Fixtures & supply**:
Plumbing fixtures and their supply or service parts, including sinks, faucets,
shower fittings, toilet parts, and appliance supply hoses.

**Valves & controls**:
Components that regulate water or gas flow, including shutoff and fill valves.
Test instruments and leak-detection materials remain distinct.

**Pipe & fittings**:
General water and gas pipe, tubing, and connecting or terminating fittings.
Identifiable drain, waste, and vent components belong with Drainage & sewer.

## Office and craft supplies

**Office supplies**:
Writing, printing, labeling, correspondence, document organization, and shipping
supplies and equipment. Jobsite measuring and marking tools remain Hand tools.

**Craft supplies**:
Materials and implements for decorative crafts, sewing, modeling, and making
small objects, including craft adhesives and transfer materials.

## Outdoor and events

**Grilling**:
Outdoor cooking equipment, fitted accessories, cooking fuels, smoking wood,
chimney starters, and grill covers. Patio furnishings and heating remain distinct.

**Gift cards**:
Prepaid merchant credit and merchant account reloads, distinct from the goods
later purchased using that credit.

**Party supplies & equipment**:
Event decorations, party accessories, disposable party service materials, and
equipment used to create event effects.

## Condiments and sauces

**Cooking oils & fats**:
Edible oils, cooking sprays, rendered fats, and lard used in cooking or seasoning.

**Nut & seed butters**:
Ready-to-use nut and seed spreads and pastes, including peanut butter and tahini.
Baking pastes such as marzipan remain baking ingredients.

**Jams & preserves**:
Sweet fruit preserves, jams, and fruit spreads.

**Vinegars**:
Culinary vinegars used for cooking and seasoning.

## Other food groups

**Candy & chocolate**:
Ready-to-eat confectionery and chocolate. Baking chocolate and meal or protein
bars remain distinct.

**Coffee & tea**:
Coffee beans, grounds, concentrates, brewed coffee, teas, and infusions.

**Frozen desserts**:
Frozen sweet desserts, including dairy and nondairy ice creams, frozen custard,
and frozen dessert sandwiches.

**Frozen fruit**:
Frozen fruit pieces, blends, pulp, and seeds.

## Powered tools and accessories

**Bench & stationary machines**:
Powered workshop machines operated on a bench, stand, or fixed work surface,
including saws, thickness planers, milling machines, lathes, and printers.

**Guides, jigs & workholding**:
Tool-specific guides, templates, fences, rail accessories, and fixturing systems.
Portable measuring instruments remain with Measuring, marking & test.

**3D printing supplies**:
Printer filament, replaceable nozzles, and related printing consumables.

**Drill bits**:
Replaceable bits for drilling, boring, and countersinking. Driver bits and router
bits remain distinct.

**Abrasives & sanding media**:
Sandpaper, abrasive discs and belts, sanding sponges, and expendable sanding
support materials.
